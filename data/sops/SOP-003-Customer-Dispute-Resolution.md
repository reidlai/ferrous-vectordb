# SOP-003: Customer Dispute Resolution & Merchant Mediation

**Document ID:** SOP-003  
**Category:** Customer Support & Merchant Settlement  
**Effective Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Target Systems:** Support Portal, Dispute Processing Engine

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure defines the guidelines for managing customer disputes, damaged goods claims, return merchandise authorizations (RMA), and merchant mediation.

---

## 2. Section 3.1: Damaged in Transit Claims & Evidence Intake

When a customer reports receiving damaged merchandise:
1. **Intake & Verification**:
   - Collect digital photographic proof of both external packaging condition and damaged merchandise within 7 days of recorded delivery.
   - Verify product serial / SKU match against Olist order item records.
2. **Immediate Resolution Workflow**:
   - For damages under R$ 50,00, offer instant replacement or store credit without requiring package return.
   - For damages over R$ 50,00, issue prepaid return shipping label via authorized carrier.

---

## 3. Section 3.2: Merchant Mediation & Chargeback Inquiries

1. **Merchant Notification**:
   - When a dispute is filed, the marketplace merchant receives an automated notification with 48 hours to provide clarification or consent to customer refund.
2. **Chargeback Prevention**:
   - Active communication must be initiated before banking chargebacks mature into formal claims.
   - If dispute is unresolved after 48 hours, customer support intervention team issues final binding determination.

---

## 4. Section 3.3: Return Package Verification & Settlement Authorization

- Returned parcels undergo physical inspection at the reverse logistics regional center within 48 hours of intake scan.
- Upon inspection approval, the refund command is dispatched to the payment gateway and recorded in the event log.
- Unjustified or fraudulent claims are recorded in the risk management database and flagged for review.
